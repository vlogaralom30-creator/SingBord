# Privacy Policy for SingBord Keyboard 🛡️

**Last Updated**: September 15, 2026  
**Effective Date**: September 15, 2026  

At **SingBord** (developed by Naxxivo Tech Lab), we believe your keystrokes are fundamentally personal. What you type — whether it's a private message to a loved one, a sensitive password, or personal financial details — should belong strictly to you, and nobody else.

This Privacy Policy outlines our unwavering commitment to your privacy.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection Pledge
- **We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data.**
- **No Keystroke Logging**: We never record, buffer, or transmit what you type.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry**: There are no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Flurry, or third-party trackers inside SingBord.
- **No Crash Trackers**: We do not send automatic diagnostic dumps to external servers.

---

## 2. Zero Network Connectivity
- SingBord does **NOT** declare or request the Android `android.permission.INTERNET` permission in `AndroidManifest.xml`.
- Because the operating system strictly enforces permissions, it is technically **impossible** for the SingBord app or keyboard service to communicate over the internet or send data to any remote server.

---

## 3. Local Storage of Preferences Only
- Any preferences you adjust within the app (such as line thickness, keyboard height, number row toggle, sound, or haptics) are stored exclusively in your device's sandboxed local `SharedPreferences`.
- This data never leaves your device and is completely deleted if you uninstall the application.

---

## 4. Android System IME Warning Explanation
When you enable any third-party keyboard on Android, the operating system displays a standard generic alert stating:  
*"This input method may be able to collect all the text you type, including personal data like passwords and credit card numbers."*

This is a mandatory system dialog that Android shows for **every single keyboard**, including Google Gboard, Samsung Keyboard, and open-source keyboards. Because SingBord has **no internet permission**, any text you type cannot physically leave your device.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services
SingBord integrates zero third-party software development kits (SDKs), advertising networks, or marketing libraries.
